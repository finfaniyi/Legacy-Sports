HOW IT WORKS

TOURNAMENT FORMAT.

We are preparing the 2027 tournament system now. Final structure, schedules and team requirements will be updated here as planning is confirmed.

01

Exhibition Round

Working format: exhibition games establish seeding before teams move into elimination play.

02

Bracket

Working format: teams advance into a seeded single-elimination bracket.

03

Match Format

  • Best of 3 sets
  • Sets 1–2: first to 15
  • Set 3: first to 11
04

Team Rules

Working roster size is 6–8 players. Final co-ed roster and on-court requirements will be confirmed for 2027.

GAME PLAY

VOLLEYBALL RULES.

The final Legacy Sports rulebook will be published before registration opens.

WORKING RULEBOOK

Subject to final 2027 confirmation.

  1. The previous format required a minimum of 6 players, including 2 female players with at least 1 female player on court.
  2. A player may not block, spike or double-fist a serve return.
  3. A serve may contact the net and remain in play if it crosses over.
  4. Players may volley a serve and volley the ball over the net without being square to its path.
  5. Open-hand tips and directs are allowed.
  6. A block touch does not count as the team's first contact, and the blocker may play the ball again.
  7. The ball may be played using any part of the body.
  8. Players may not touch the net during play.
  9. A ball touching a boundary line is in.
  10. Catching, holding or throwing the ball is illegal.
  11. After the serve, front-line players may switch positions at the net.
  12. Teams rotate clockwise after winning the serve back.
  13. Substitution rules will be confirmed in the final 2027 rulebook.
  14. Referee decisions are final.
DETAILS ARE TENTATIVE.

July is the planned event month. The 2026 tournament format is being used as a working foundation, but the final 2027 date, venue, price, prize and rules are not confirmed yet.
