3v3 Basketball Tournament

The 3v3 Basketball Tournament marked the beginning of Legacy Sports. The tournament set the tone with intense competition, electric energy, and a strong sense of community that filled the court from start to finish.

2026

Coed Volleyball Tournament

The Volleyball Tournament marked the rebrand of Legacy Sports and welcomed over 171 attendees for a day packed with excitement, competition, and community engagement. While teams battled it out on the court, guests enjoyed a variety of interactive activities including trivia booths, photo booths, giveaways, and crowd engagement games. The event combined high-level competition with a fun and energetic atmosphere, creating an unforgettable experience that reflected the new direction and vision of Legacy Sports.

The 2026 Winners:Bratz

Bratz defeated Smurfs to be Crowned your 2026 Volleyball Champions which got them the $400 Cash Prize and Gold Place Medal and the Trophy.
