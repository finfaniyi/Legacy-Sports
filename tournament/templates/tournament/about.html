Who are we

Legacy Sports is a Canadian community sports organization focused on creating inclusive opportunities for individuals to stay active, connect, and feel part of something. We design and run tournaments and social sporting events that bring people together in a welcoming, low-pressure environment, where all skill levels are encouraged to show up and play. Not everyone is here to compete at a high level, and that’s the point. Some come to stay active, some to meet new people, and others just to have fun. Legacy Sports exists to create a space where all of that belongs.

Founder

Why I Started Legacy Sports

I was 14 when I moved to Canada and started high school. Everything felt new. At that age, it was overwhelming. I was trying to find my place, build friendships, and figure out where I belonged… and then COVID hit, making everything even more isolating.

In the last year and a half of high school, things began to shift. I met people who showed me that you’re never truly alone, and that the right community can make all the difference. For the first time, I felt what it meant to be part of something.

I have always loved sports and over time I explored others, such as volleyball and frisbee. Through sport, I found something I had been searching for. A way to connect. A way to belong.

When I started university, I found myself back in that same uncertain place. But everything began to change when I joined intramurals and clubs. I met people who became real friends and once again found a sense of community that changed everything.

That is why I created Legacy Sports.

Legacy Sports is built on a simple belief that sport has the power to bring people together. Not just through competition, but connection. It builds confidence, creates real friendships, and gives people a sense of belonging.

You do not need to be an athlete to be part of it. It is not about skill or experience. It is about showing up, being included, and having a space where you belong.

Legacy Sports is about more than just sport. It is about community, connection, and redefining what it means to be part of sport.

Our Vision for the Community

Our vision is to build a thriving, inclusive sports community that brings together individuals of all backgrounds and skill levels. We aim to create a space where people can compete, connect, and feel a true sense of belonging whether they are chasing excellence, staying active, or simply enjoying the game.

Our Mission

Legacy Sports is committed to creating inclusive sporting experiences where individuals feel a genuine sense of belonging, stay active, and build meaningful social connections. Through both recreational and competitive events, we promote mental well-being, community, and memorable experiences that leave a lasting impact.

Giving Back & Creative Opportunities

Legacy Sports is committed to giving back to the community by creating opportunities for young individuals to grow, contribute, and gain real-world experience. We actively welcome high school students who are looking to complete volunteer hours, as well as individuals who have an interest in creative fields such as photography, videography, and content creation. Whether someone is just starting out or looking to develop their skills, we aim to provide a space where they can learn, experiment, and build confidence through real event experience. Beyond creative roles, we also offer opportunities for individuals to take on responsibilities in helping run events. This includes areas such as coordination, team support, and on-site operations, allowing participants to develop valuable skills in communication, teamwork, and leadership. Legacy Sports is not just about playing. It’s about creating opportunities for people to grow, connect, and be part of something meaningful.
