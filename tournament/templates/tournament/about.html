WHO WE ARE

MORE THAN
THE GAME.

Legacy Sports is a Canadian community sports organization focused on creating inclusive opportunities for individuals to stay active, connect, and feel part of something.

We design tournaments and social sporting events that bring people together in a welcoming, low-pressure environment where every skill level is encouraged to show up and play.

Some come to compete. Some come to stay active. Some come to meet new people. Others just want to have fun. There is space for all of it here.

THE STORY BEHIND LEGACY

WHY I STARTED
LEGACY SPORTS

I was 14 when I moved to Canada and started high school. Everything felt new. I was trying to find my place, build friendships, and figure out where I belonged. Then COVID hit, making everything even more isolating.

In the last year and a half of high school, things began to shift. I met people who showed me that you are never truly alone, and that the right community can make all the difference.

I have always loved sports and, over time, explored others such as volleyball and frisbee. Through sport, I found something I had been searching for: a way to connect and a way to belong.

When I started university, intramurals and clubs helped me find that sense of community again.

THAT IS WHY I CREATED LEGACY SPORTS.

Legacy Sports is built on a simple belief that sport has the power to bring people together — not just through competition, but through connection, confidence, friendships, and belonging.

You do not need to be an athlete to be part of it. It is not about skill or experience. It is about showing up, being included, and having a space where you belong.

01 OUR VISION

BUILD A COMMUNITY THAT PEOPLE WANT TO BE PART OF.

Our vision is to build a thriving, inclusive sports community that brings together individuals of all backgrounds and skill levels — whether they are chasing excellence, staying active, or simply enjoying the game.

02 OUR MISSION

CREATE EXPERIENCES THAT LEAVE A LASTING IMPACT.

We create inclusive recreational and competitive sporting experiences where people can stay active, build meaningful social connections, and feel a genuine sense of belonging.

BEYOND SPORT

OPPORTUNITIES
TO GROW.

Legacy Sports is committed to giving back by creating opportunities for young people to grow, contribute, and gain real-world experience.

We welcome high school students completing volunteer hours and people interested in photography, videography, content creation, event coordination, team support, and on-site operations.

Legacy Sports is not just about playing. It is about creating opportunities for people to grow, connect, contribute, and be part of something meaningful.
